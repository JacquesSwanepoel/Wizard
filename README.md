# Wizard 2023
A magic based fps I tried to develop.

I was inspired by a game I played "Gauntlet" and its magic system. The system is that you combine two of three different magic elements, and a resulting spell could be cast from the combination.

I was also at the time, part of a DnD campaign where I played a wizard named Wizardo the foolish, a chaotic degenerate wizard.

I used the parts of the character controller I developed for Gun Gun and Bludlust for this project aswell.

This project didnt get far into development, I did manage to prototype the main mechanic of spell casting, but lost motivation after realizing how much work visual assets would take me to create. I did however also become more profecient with blender as a result.
